The 55-page PDF is the correct Congressional Record segment (Senate, 9 Feb 1955). text_en.md contains pp. 1380-1382, re-extracted column by column from the OCR layer: Sen. George's statement for the Committee on Foreign Relations, with "THE LEGAL STATUS OF FORMOSA" on p. 1381 quoting the understanding "nothing in the present treaty shall be construed as affecting or modifying the legal status or the sovereignty of the territories referred to in article VI" and the other two understandings. Rest of the debate (pp. 1362-1416) is in the PDF only. The underlying S. Exec. Rept. No. 2, 84th Cong., 1st Sess. (cited p. 6 in the 1971 memo) was not found online (searched govinfo/HathiTrust/web; printed in Executive Sessions of the Senate Foreign Relations Committee vol. VII pp. 774-781 per FRUS 1955-57 vol. II). OCR errors are left as in source.

Added 2026-10-16: the full PDF shows Sen. George stating the understandings "are not reservations" and "not technically inserted in the resolution of ratification", but "tantamount to reservations" with "a high degree of solemnity"; the resolution of ratification as read by the Chief Clerk contains no understanding; a proposed reservation was rejected; a dissenting senator said the understandings would have "absolutely no legal effect" before an international court. Sen. Holland's question refers to "the Republic of China, now occupying that area".
