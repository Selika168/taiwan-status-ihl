Promulgated 1950-05-04 (replacing the 1899 Law No. 66, P27). Art. 1: 「日本国民たる要件は、この法律の定めるところによる。」 Loss of nationality is provided only in Arts. 8 (voluntary acquisition of a foreign nationality), 9 (failure to reserve nationality on birth abroad) and 10 (renunciation by a national who holds another nationality). The Act has no provision for loss by territorial change or by peace treaty. Relevance: the 1952 circular (P46) declared Formosans' loss of nationality on a ground the Act does not contain; Constitution Art. 10 (P102) reserves the matter to law. Counter-view: the Supreme Court held that the loss followed from the peace treaties themselves (P53; see also P44).
