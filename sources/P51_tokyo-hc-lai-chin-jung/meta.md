UN summary and extracts of the decision, reported at X Tokyo Koto Saibansho Keiji Hanketsu Jiho 473 (東京高等裁判所判決時報(刑事) vol. 10 p. 473): Formosa and the Pescadores came to belong to the ROC at any rate on 1952-08-05 (Treaty of Taipei); Formosans holding ROC nationality under ROC law lost Japanese nationality; the Court cites the ROC 'Ordinance for Determination of Nationality of the Formosans residing abroad' (cf. P34) for the restoration of Chinese nationality from 1945-10-25. Only the UN's English summary/extract (printed pp. 68-71 of the UN volume) was obtained; the full judgment text and the Japanese original were NOT found in open sources (web searches of courts.go.jp hanrei and general search found nothing; the 下級裁判所/行政事件裁判例集/高刑集 reporters are not freely online). The four-page PDF excerpt contains pdf pages 96-99 of the UN volume, covering this and neighbouring Formosan cases. Reported in ILR and the Japanese Annual of International Law (not accessible without paywalled access).
