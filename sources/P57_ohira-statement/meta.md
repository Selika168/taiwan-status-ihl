Verified and saved the complete record of the joint press conference of Foreign Minister Ohira and Chief Cabinet Secretary Nikaido after signing of the Japan-PRC Joint Communique, in Japanese. The key passage is in Ohira's concluding remarks: as a result of normalization, the Japan-ROC Peace Treaty 'has lost its meaning to continue to exist and is regarded as terminated' (日華平和条約は、存続の意義を失い、終了したものと認められる). Also contains his explanation of paragraph 3 of the Communique on Taiwan and the Q&A on closing the Japan-Taiwan diplomatic relations. The statement was oral; the MOFA publication of the same press conference (外務省 'わが外交の近況' / 日中関係基本資料集) is the printed source used by the database. No English version saved.
