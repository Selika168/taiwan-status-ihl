UN summary and extracts of the decision, reported at IX Gyosei Jiken Saiban Rei Shu 2087 (行政事件裁判例集 vol. 9 p. 2087): Court held Japan had already renounced sovereignty over Formosa by accepting the Potsdam Declaration / Instrument of Surrender, and that Formosans lost Japanese nationality by the surrender, subject to the incoming-husband (nyufu) analogy under Nationality Law Art. 5(2). Only the UN's English summary/extract (printed pp. 68-71 of the UN volume) was obtained; the full judgment text and the Japanese original were NOT found in open sources (web searches of courts.go.jp hanrei and general search found nothing; the 下級裁判所/行政事件裁判例集/高刑集 reporters are not freely online). The four-page PDF excerpt contains pdf pages 96-99 of the UN volume, covering this and neighbouring Formosan cases. Reported in ILR and the Japanese Annual of International Law (not accessible without paywalled access).
