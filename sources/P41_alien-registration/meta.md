The ordinance is Imperial Ordinance (勅令) No. 207 of 1947-05-02, not No. 206 as the item list says; the citing document's 'Edict no. 206' appears to be an error. Saved the full ordinance (including Art. 11: Formosans designated by the Home Minister and Koreans are deemed aliens for the time being) in Japanese (kyujitai and shinjitai) and in the contemporary English translation. Repealed 1952-04-28 by Law No. 125 (Alien Registration Law). NOT retrieved: the text of the Enforcement Regulations (内務省令第28号, 1947); only the NDL 日本法令索引 metadata page was saved (reference_regulations.html) and the NDL 官報 text layer is not accessible (403/404), and web search found no transcription. Hence partial. Codex's earlier placeholders (a 404 page saved as OCR html and an HTML error saved as .pdf) were deleted.
