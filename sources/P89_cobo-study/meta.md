Paras 379-382 working definition: 'historical continuity with pre-invasion and pre-colonial societies'; para 380: continuity 'may consist of ... one or more of' ancestral lands, common ancestry, culture, language, residence, other; para 381: self-identification + acceptance by the group; para 382: the community's 'sovereign right and power to decide who belongs to them'. Language is one optional factor, not a requirement.
