2006 answer (Koizumi Cabinet): the position is based on Joint Communique para. 3 and Potsdam para. 8 "which Japan has accepted"; and 「昭和四十七年の日中共同声明は、法的拘束力を有するものではない」. 2022 question (Wada Yuichiro) asked directly whether 「十分理解し、尊重」 means 「承認」, and recited the government view that Japan, having renounced under SFPT Art. 2, 「台湾の領土的な位置づけに関しての独自の認定を行う立場にはない」. 2022 answer (Kishida Cabinet) did not say "recognize": the position is to 「十分理解し、尊重する」 the PRC position; declined to answer the alleged-deviation question as unclear.
