Institutional history published by a prosecutors' office of the present administering authority (PDF created 2019-07-03, 14 pages). Points used:
- Military-government period 1895-05-08 to 1896-03-31: under the 1895-06-28 地方官假官制, civil cases were decided by the county 內務部 and criminal cases by the county 警察部 (i.e. by administrative and police organs). (The PDF misprints the year as 1985.)
- 1896-05-01: 臺灣總督府法院條例 issued as 律令第一號 (Ritsurei No. 1); courts in three levels (地方、覆審、高等), 「三級三審」; judges and prosecutors appointed by the Governor-General, judges required to hold 判事 qualification.
- 1896-07-11 律令第二號: 臺灣總督府臨時法院 for political cases, single instance; the 匪徒刑罰令 with death as the main penalty.
- Conflict between Governor-General Nogi and Chief Judge Takano Moritake: Takano was removed from the court by police; of 16 qualified judges, 14 resigned or were dismissed within a year.
- Later reforms (1919): the Governor-General lost the power to suspend judges; the 臨時法院 for political offenders was abolished.
Secondary source, not the ordinance itself. Complements P99 (Judicial Yuan): together they give the 1896-05-01 ordinance, the 1896-07-15 start of the courts, and the limits on judicial independence.
