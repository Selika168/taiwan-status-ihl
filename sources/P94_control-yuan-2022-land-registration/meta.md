Nature: official finding of the ROC's own oversight organ. The petitioners' names are redacted in the original (蕭○旺).

Key passages:
- 1946-04-05 notice 卯微卅五署民字第2896號: declaration window 「自本年4月21日起至5月20日止，在此1個月期間內」.
- 臺灣地籍釐整辦法 (Executive Yuan, 1946-11-26 / promulgated 1946-12-03) Art. 4: 「在光復前日本政府已辦不動產登記之區域，不動產權利人應將所持登記證書向主管地政機關繳驗……」.
- 臺灣省土地權利憑證繳驗及換發權利書狀辦法 (1947-05-02) Art. 14: 「逾期無人申請驗證之土地，或經申請而逾限未補繳證件者，其土地視為無主土地，由該管縣市地政機關公告2個月，公告期滿，無人提出異議者，即為國有土地。」 Executive Yuan order of 1947-07-10: custody by the land authority, State registration after two years.
- Land Act Art. 57 (as amended 1946-04-29) carried a rule designed for clearing public land on the mainland into Taiwan, where registration already existed.
- Staffing and language: Taitung land-adjustment office, June 1946: 「本縣高山族同胞，幾佔半數，既不通臺語，更不諳漢文，工作頗感困難」.
- Later remedies: 1990 national land-administration conference; 地籍清理條例 (2007, in force 2008-07-01); 國有財產法 Art. 52-2 (added 2000-01-12) — people may **buy** land used for building/residence since before 1946-12-31 at the first announced land value — 「足資證明光復初期辦理權利憑證繳驗問題重重，確有疏失」.
- Finding: denying restoration under Art. 14 and the 1973 Executive Yuan principle 「甚難通過『憲法』保障財產權之檢驗」; the government should review.
