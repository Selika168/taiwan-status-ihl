Full text of the written answer. Key phrases: Cairo "a statement of intention"; "This retrocession has, in fact, never taken place"; 1945 takeover "at the direction of the Supreme Commander of the Allied Powers; but this was not a cession, nor did it in itself involve any change of sovereignty"; "on a basis of military occupation pending further arrangements"; 1952 renunciation "did not operate as a transfer to Chinese sovereignty, whether to the People's Republic of China or to the Chinese Nationalist authorities"; "territory the de jure sovereignty over which is uncertain or undetermined". Offshore islands distinguished as "undoubtedly" PRC territory. A government statement of position, not a judicial ruling.
