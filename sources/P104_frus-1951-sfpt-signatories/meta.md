Lists the 48 Allied signatories and Japan (signature at the San Francisco Opera House, 1951-09-08). Neither the Republic of China nor the People's Republic of China is among them (cf. SFPT Arts. 21, 25, P04). The signature pages in the UNTS reproduction of P04 are illegible OCR; this note is the clean list. Full signer names: Department of State, Treaty of Peace: Proceedings, pp. 313-455, or 3 UST 3169. Signature is not ratification: which signatories ratified is not in this source.
