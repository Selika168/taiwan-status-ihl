The depositary record keeps one entry, "China": Signature 10.12.1949 (signed by the delegation of the Republic of China, cf. P105, which lists the same date and "尚未批准"), Ratification 28.12.1956 and reservations of the same date, made in the name of "the People's Republic of China" (UNTS vol. 260, 1957, pp. 438-444). There is no separate signature by the PRC and no accession: the PRC ratified on the 1949 signature (cf. ICRC 2025 commentary on Art. 155, para. 6950: ratification is open only to signatories; accession to non-signatories — commentary not reproduced, ICRC copyright).
