Reinscription precedent: preamble takes note of the Assembly of French Polynesia resolution (18 Aug 2011) and the Council of Ministers decision (15 Jun 2011) asking for reinscription, and of support by Pacific States, Polynesian Leaders Group, Pacific Islands Forum and NAM; op. 1 'recognizes that French Polynesia remains a Non-Self-Governing Territory' and 'declares that an obligation exists under Article 73 e ... on the part of the Government of France'. Related: A/RES/41/41 (1986, New Caledonia) stored as extra file.
