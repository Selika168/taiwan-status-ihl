The 4.8 MB English PDF is the whole UN Legislative Series volume 14 (272 pp.), not the circular alone. The circular's English text (item (1) Korea and Formosa, sub-items i-v, including 'all Koreans and Formosans including those residing in Japan proper shall lose their Japanese nationality, ipso facto') as reproduced by the UN is extracted to text_en.md. The UN reproduces only that part (with its own ellipsis); the full circular (it also covers other sections e.g. persons of Japanese origin, naturalization procedure) and the Japanese original (法務省民事甲第438号 民事局長通達 昭和27年4月19日) were NOT found in open online sources; searches of NDL/courts.go.jp/web search only returned commentary. Also see P44, P50, P51 which are extracted from the same volume. Suggested next step: Minji-geppo (1952) or 法務省 戸籍 circular compilations in NDL Digital Collections.
