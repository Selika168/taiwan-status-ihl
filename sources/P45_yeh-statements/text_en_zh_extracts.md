# Foreign Minister Yeh Kung-chao (葉公超) - statements to the Legislative Yuan on the San Francisco and Sino-Japanese peace treaties, July 1952 (EXTRACTS ONLY)

Extracts copied verbatim from the compilation page "Reference / 各國意見" of the Republic of Taiwan & Penghu Network (secondary source; saved as reference_links.html). Each item shows the compiler's citation. These are quotations, not full statements; the full statements were not found. The English quotes derive from a US Embassy Taipei despatch summarizing/translating Yeh's remarks.

## 1. 1952-07 - 於立法院就「中日和平條約」案提出補充說明 (supplementary explanation on the Sino-Japanese Peace Treaty)

「中日和約對於日本前所占我之領土，因格於金山和約之規定，除規定由日本予以放棄外，並未明定其歸還我國。」

Citation given: 中華民國外交問題研究會《中華民國對日和約》，臺北市：中國國民黨中央委員會黨史委員會，1966年9月，頁348。

「惟在法律上因金山和約及中日和約均未明定台灣、澎湖之歸屬，故有此項補充規定之必要。」

Citation given: same book, 頁349。

## 2. 1952-07-23 - 葉公超於立法院答覆關於舊金山和約及台北和約的質詢 (reply to interpellation on the SF Treaty and the Treaty of Taipei; as reported in US Embassy Taipei Despatch No. 31 to the Department of State, 23 July 1952, Enclosure 2)

"under the San Francisco Peace Treaty "no provision was made for the return [of these islands] to China. Inasmuch as these territories were originally owned by us and as they are now under our control and, furthermore, Japan has renounced in the Sino-Japanese peace treaty these territories under the San Francisco Treaty of Peace, they are, therefore, in fact restored to us."

Citation given: Despatch No.31 from the American Embassy in Taipei to the Department of State, July 23, 1952, Enclosure 2, at p.1.

"In fact, we are controlling them now, and undoubtedly they constitute a part of our territories. However, the delicate international situation makes it that they do not belong to us. Under present circumstances, Japan has no right to transfer Formosa and the Pescadores to us; nor can we accept such a transfer from Japan even if she so wishes..."

Citation given: Despatch No.31 ..., July 23, 1952, Enclosure 2, at p.2.
