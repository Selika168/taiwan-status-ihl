Only extracts were obtained: two excerpts of Yeh's July 1952 supplementary explanation to the Legislative Yuan (quoted from 中華民國外交問題研究會《中華民國對日和約》1966, pp. 348-349) and two quotations from his 23 July 1952 reply to interpellation as recorded in US Embassy Taipei Despatch No. 31, Enclosure 2 (not located in FRUS or open archives). They are saved in text_en_zh_extracts.md and include the sentence cited in the item list that no provision for return of Taiwan and Penghu was made in the SF Treaty. The full statement texts (立法院公報 vol. 1952, 立法院議事錄, 外交部 file 對日和約) were not found online; the 國家檔案 file has page images behind a captcha download, so it was not fetched (no workaround attempted). The catalogue record page URL was not recorded by the earlier worker (the saved HTML has no canonical URL); the first source URL is the archive's catalogue domain only. Web searches for the original Chinese text in 立法院公報 found nothing.
