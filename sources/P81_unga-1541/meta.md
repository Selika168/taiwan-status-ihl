Two-page GAOR print containing Res. 1541 (Principles I-XII) and the start of Res. 1542. Principle IV: prima facie obligation for a territory "geographically separate and ... distinct ethnically and/or culturally from the country administering it"; Principle V: administrative, political, juridical, economic or historical elements placing it "arbitrarily ... in a position or status of subordination"; Principle VI: independence / free association / integration; Principle IX: integration via "informed and democratic processes, impartially conducted and based on universal adult suffrage". Res. 1542: the GA itself declared Portuguese "overseas provinces" non-self-governing although Portugal refused to transmit information.
