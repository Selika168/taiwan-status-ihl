Reports the treaty, agreement and exchange of notes signed at Chungking on 1946-02-28 between the ROC Foreign Minister and the French Ambassador (Meyrier). Relaying Central News Agency: the exchange of notes on Chinese forces in Indochina provides "for assumption by French command of responsibility for guarding Jap prisoners, maintenance, of peace and order, and protection of Chinese nationals north of 16 degrees north latitude and for withdrawal of Chinese forces not later than, March 31, 1946."
Limits: a contemporaneous summary of a press report, not the treaty text. Footnote: texts published in the Journal officiel (Paris) of 19 May 1946. The agreement on Sino-Indochinese relations is registered at the UN (UNTS vol. 14, p. 137, No. 216); the exchange of letters on the relief of Chinese troops appears in the UN monthly statement of April 1948 — add the UNTS texts when retrieved.
Relevance (ref 03, Ep 3): zone (a) of General Order No. 1 (P33) covered "Formosa and French Indo-China north of 16 north latitude" in one clause; the ROC handed over its zone north of 16°N to France by agreement and withdrew, while it stayed in Formosa.
