IDENTIFICATION: no FRUS 1946 VIII document dated 26 Oct 1946 deals with Taiwanese nationality (docs 255-270 scanned for Formosa/Taiwan). The only statement that Taiwanese retain Japanese nationality is doc. 276 (21 Nov 1946, aide-memoire, p. 358-359) -- the same document as P38 and P02, so P38, P39 and P02 are ONE document and the 16 Oct / 26 Oct dates are probably mis-citations (alternatively 26 Oct is a mis-dating of doc 269, memo of 25 Oct 1946 on aliens in Japan, which speaks of "Formosan-Chinese" and does not say Japanese nationals). text_en.md is the doc 276 text; text_en_d269_alt.md is saved as the alternative for the record. Marked partial because the exact intended 26 Oct document could not be confirmed.
