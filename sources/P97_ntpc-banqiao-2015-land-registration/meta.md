Nature: research report written by staff of an ROC land office, published by the New Taipei City Government. It relies mainly on 李志殷, 台灣光復初期土地權利憑證繳驗工作之研究 (MA thesis, National Chengchi University, 2003) and on an interview with a former office director. It is the land administration's own account, not a court finding.

Key passages:
- Japanese era: 1899 Taiwan Real Property Registration Rules (律令第12號): 「關於不動產之登記，由地方法院或其出張所辦理之」; 1905 Taiwan Land Registration Rules (律令第3號); after the Japanese Civil Code and Real Property Registration Act applied, registration was by opposability (登記對抗主義), so ledgers and registers drifted apart from actual title.
- Declaration window as stated here: 「自民國 35 年 4 月 21 日起至同年 5 月 21 日止，在此一個月期限內」 (P94 quotes the notice as ending 20 May — check against the Gazette).
- **The ROC's own Land Administration Department (地政署) found the Governor-General's Office procedure unlawful**: 「台灣行政長官公署依前揭等行政命令，所辦理之地籍清理，係違反當時土地法相關之規定」; it ordered by telegram of 1946-08-16 (京籍字地一四八號代電) that rules first be drafted and approved by the Executive Yuan; elsewhere: 「不符我國土地登記法體制；嗣被地政署糾正」.
- **The procedure ran before its legal basis existed**: the 1947 measure was passed by the Executive Yuan on 1947-03-25 and promulgated on 1947-05-02, by which time 「台灣地區有關自然人權利憑證繳驗，審查工作已告完畢，部分縣市正進行至土地權利公告接近完成之階段；且亦有部分縣市，已完成登記工作」.
- Court registry gap: the Japanese-era registers passed to the courts, but 「出張所之組織為我國法院組織法所無」; the Taiwan High Court made its own interim rules; the branch registries were then turned into land offices.
- Land Act Art. 57 (as amended 1946-04-29): undeclared land deemed ownerless and registered as State land; the report: 「採取之手段卻是剝奪其財產權，無償收歸國有」, and doubts its constitutionality under the proportionality principle.
- Defects recorded: no transition rules between mainland and Taiwan property law; staff quality, language barrier, incomplete and fast-changing rules; Japanese-era unregistered transfers by agreement sometimes not accepted (「甚至有不受理日據時期因意思合致而取得物權但未為登記者之申請的情形發生」); review rules only issued around end of August 1946.
- Conclusion: 「更有將臺灣人民私有土地所有權收歸國有之情形發生」; the verification 「對某些日據時期擁有土地財產權之台灣人民，造成不當之侵害」; 「有違憲法保障人民財產權之意旨」.
- Case study: land whose ledger showed a 1943 transfer from the Treasury to a private owner was declared by the Treasury (國庫) in 1946 and registered to it; the registrar left the entry unsealed with the note 「查臺帳褚風來」.
- Recommendations: training of land-office staff on the background of the general registration; interpretive rulings and legislation; until then, court action.
