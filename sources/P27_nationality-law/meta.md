Saved the full Japanese text of the Nationality Law (Arts. 1-28, promulgated 1899-03-16, in force 1899-04-01; repealed 1950-05-04 by Law No. 147) and of Imperial Ordinance No. 289 (promulgated 1899-06-21) which put Law No. 66 into force in Taiwan, each in original kyujitai and in the site's shinjitai rendering. Taiwan extension is therefore dated 1899, not 1902; the 'implemented in 1902' phrase in the citing document is not confirmed by these instruments (the Nationality Law also contains no Taiwan-specific rules; the 1902 date may relate to a different measure and was not located). The National Archives page is the signed original's catalogue/viewer record, with no machine-readable text. No official e-Gov text exists for this repealed law. Related Taiwan nationality measures (e.g. 1897 option period under the Treaty of Shimonoseki) are covered under P26.
