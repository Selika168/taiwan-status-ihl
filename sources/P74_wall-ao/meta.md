text_en.md extracts paras 90-101. Key: para 91 — Switzerland as depositary considered Palestine's unilateral undertaking of 7 June 1982 to apply GC IV valid, but was "not - as a depositary - in a position to decide whether" the 14 June 1989 accession request "can be considered as an instrument of accession". Para 95 — Art. 2(2) "is not to restrict the scope of application ... by excluding therefrom territories not falling under the sovereignty of one of the contracting parties"; drafters sought protection "regardless of the status of the occupied territories, as is shown by Article 47". Para 101 — applies "there being no need for any enquiry into the precise prior status of those territories" (answer to the 'missing reversioner' argument).
