In force 1947-05-03; never amended. Art. 10: 「日本国民たる要件は、法律でこれを定める。」 / "The conditions necessary for being a Japanese national shall be determined by law." Cited in ref 02 against the 1952 loss of nationality by administrative circular (P46). Art. 98(2) (treaties to be faithfully observed) is the basis for the counter-view that the loss followed from the peace treaty itself (P53 majority).
