The French translation (pp. 229-) is in the volume PDF but not extracted. Note for the citation audit: Art. VI as signed only defines "territorial/territories" (Taiwan and the Pescadores) and contains NO "legal status or sovereignty" wording; the quoted sentence ("nothing in the present treaty shall be construed as affecting or modifying the legal status or the sovereignty...") is the Senate Foreign Relations Committee understanding, see P49 (101 Cong. Rec. 1381).
