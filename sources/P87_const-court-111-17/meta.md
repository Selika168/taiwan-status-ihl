Wikisource transcription (official PDFs at cons.judicial.gov.tw). Holding 1: constitutionally protected indigenous peoples 'include all Austronesian peoples existing in Taiwan'; other Austronesian peoples whose language, customs, traditions still survive, whose members maintain group identity, and with objective historical records may apply. Holding 3: 3-year deadline; failing legislation, persons whose Japanese-era household register (戶口調查簿) marks self or lineal ascendant as 「熟」 or 「平」 may apply. Para [14]: Japanese-era registers classified 「生」「高砂」 vs 「熟」「平」 by region and degree of assimilation. Para [36]: peoples fully assimilated and having lost their culture are not the specially protected indigenous peoples.
