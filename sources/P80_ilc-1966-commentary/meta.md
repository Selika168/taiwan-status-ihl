Whole 89-page PDF saved; text_en.md extracts draft Art. 70 commentary (two-column, unaligned). Key: draft Art. 49 (= VCLT 52) voids only treaties procured by force "in violation of the principles of the Charter", and "a treaty provision imposed on an aggressor State would not therefore infringe article 49"; Art. 70 (= VCLT 75) is a general reservation also covering obligations for an aggressor as a third State (draft Art. 31 = VCLT 35).
