Holding (主文 1): land owned by the people in the Japanese era, registered as State land because not registered within the general-registration deadline — the owner's claim to cancel the State registration is **not subject to extinctive prescription**; Supreme Court precedent 70-Tai-Shang-311 is unconstitutional in that part (Constitution Art. 15, property).

Reasons quoted in the summary (paras 16–17, 25): Land Act Art. 43 「依本法所為之登記，有絕對效力」 protects third parties who rely on the register; it does not take the true owner's right; registration only presumes ownership. The post-1945 general registration 「原則上承認人民日治時期之土地所有權」 and was a procedure of clearing records.
