Official exhibit page of the present administering authority's judiciary, captioning Japanese-era court archives. Main points: the Governor-General's courts, "專掌司法審判權", began operating on 1896-07-15; the 覆審法院 was the court of last instance for civil and criminal cases in the early period; the Governor-General held supervisory power over judicial administration and initiated disciplinary proceedings against judges, which the page calls 「對法官獨立審判的威脅」; Chief Judge Takano Moritake was dismissed in 1897 while trying a corruption case against senior officials (「並未有足夠之身分保障」); in the late war period prosecutors reported to the Governor-General, but 「審判系統仍就個案自為裁判」.
This is a secondary, institutional summary, not the 1896 ordinance itself (臺灣總督府法院條例, 律令 1896). The ordinance text has not been located online; add it as a separate source when found.
