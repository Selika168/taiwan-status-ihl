The order is a single sentence; its full text is quoted from the secondary source newtalk.tw (text_zh.md section A). The primary image kept is the 長官公署民政處 relay draft (document no. 824, type 訓令, to all county/city governments) that cites 'Executive Yuan 35-01-12 Jie-san No. 01297' and abbreviates the quoted order with an ellipsis; its handwriting was transcribed by eye (section B, unproofread; Tesseract was unusable). The number reads 01297 in the Taiwan Historica article, in the draft, and in newtalk (the '節參字第0397號' variant in the items list was not seen). Date: the Executive Yuan order is dated 1946-01-12 (not 01-10). The Executive Yuan's own original, and the Taiwan Provincial Gazette printing, were not retrieved; hence partial.
