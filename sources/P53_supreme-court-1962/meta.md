Verified: the 1962-12-05 Grand Bench judgment is Showa 33 (A) 2109, an Alien Registration Law case, dismissing the appeal and holding, following the 1961-04-05 Grand Bench Kanda decision (Showa 30 (O) 890; Minshu 15-4-657, Korean legal status), that persons with Taiwanese legal status (registered in the Taiwan family register, or removed from the Japanese register through marriage to a Taiwanese) lost Japanese nationality on 1952-08-05 when the Japan-ROC Treaty of Peace entered into force, Taiwan having been transferred by that treaty. Justice Okuno Kenichi concurred separately arguing Japan renounced Taiwan on accepting the Potsdam Declaration and that the Common Law (共通法) was ineffective. The full judgment and the supplementary opinion are saved from the unofficial transcription; the official courts.go.jp hanrei record could not be located (the site search is JavaScript-driven and web searches did not surface it); the reporter citation (刑集16巻12号1661頁) is from general knowledge and not confirmed here.
