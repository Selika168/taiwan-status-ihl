IDENTIFICATION: the quoted sentence ("these persons throughout the war were enemy nationals and according to Japanese law still retain Japanese nationality") and the page number p. 358 match FRUS 1946 VIII doc. 276, the State Dept aide-memoire of 21 Nov 1946 (same as P02). The 16 Oct date appears to be a citation error: the FRUS 1946 VIII volume index lists "Taiwanese in Japan, status of, 358" only; the two documents dated 16 Oct 1946 (docs 263, 264) are unrelated (Inter-Allied Trade Board; Atcheson telegram) and none of docs 255-270 (Oct 1946) concerns Formosan nationality. Text is therefore a copy of P02. The pg_358 lookup is kept in _lookups/.
