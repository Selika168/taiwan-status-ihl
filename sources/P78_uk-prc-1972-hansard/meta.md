Full statement, Q&A and communique. Communique: UK "acknowledging the position of the Chinese Government that Taiwan is a province of the People's Republic of China" (participle) vs "recognise the Government of the PRC as the sole legal Government of China". Douglas-Home in answer: "We held the view both at Cairo and at Potsdam that Taiwan should be restored to China. That view has not changed. We think that the Taiwan question is China's internal affair to be settled by the Chinese people themselves." Also: "Our consul was never accredited to the Government of Taiwan. He was accredited to the local provincial authorities." Asked about the wishes of the people of Taiwan: "I do not have any indication of the wishes of the people of Taiwan."
