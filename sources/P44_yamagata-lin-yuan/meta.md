UN summary of the decision, reported at II Ka Min Shu 1075 (下級裁判所民事裁判例集 vol. 2 p. 1075): Formosans, after surrender, placed outside Japanese sovereignty and may be treated as aliens in some cases, but as sovereignty over Formosa is not finally determined, private-law personal-status matters are decided under existing Japanese law. Only the UN's English summary/extract (printed pp. 68-71 of the UN volume) was obtained; the full judgment text and the Japanese original were NOT found in open sources (web searches of courts.go.jp hanrei and general search found nothing; the 下級裁判所/行政事件裁判例集/高刑集 reporters are not freely online). The four-page PDF excerpt contains pdf pages 96-99 of the UN volume, covering this and neighbouring Formosan cases. Reported in ILR and the Japanese Annual of International Law (not accessible without paywalled access).
