# Notice on source texts

`sources/` contains text extracts (not the original files) of: treaties and official instruments; UK Parliamentary records (Hansard, Open Parliament Licence v3.0); US government records (FRUS, Congressional Record, court opinions — public domain); Japanese statutes, judgments and Cabinet answers (not subject to copyright under Art. 13 of the Japanese Copyright Act); Taiwanese statutes and judgments (Art. 9 of the Taiwan Copyright Act); UN and ICJ documents; ICTY judgments. Each `meta.md` gives the source URL; consult the original for authoritative text.

Not included: ICRC commentaries (Pictet) — cited by page with a link; secondary scholarship and news — cited only.

If you hold rights in any text included here and object to its inclusion, open an issue and it will be removed.
