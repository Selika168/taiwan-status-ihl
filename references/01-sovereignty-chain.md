# 01 — The sovereignty chain, 1895–1972

Each link: what the text says → what it proves → limits. `Pxx` = folder in `sources/`.

| # | Date | Instrument | Text (quote) | Proves | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 1895 | Treaty of Shimonoseki Arts. 2(b), 5 (P98) | Art. 2 "China cedes to Japan in perpetuity and full sovereignty"; Art. 5 two years to sell property and leave, after which those remaining "shall, at the option of Japan, be deemed to be Japanese subjects" | cession **with an option of nationality**; Japan's own practice | no general rule that every transfer must give an option |
| L2 | 1943 | Cairo Declaration (P28) | "Formosa, and the Pescadores, shall be restored to the Republic of China" | statement of intent | unsigned communiqué; Eden 1955: "a statement of intention" |
| L3 | 1945-07 / 09-02 | Potsdam para. 8 (P30); Instrument of Surrender (P32) | "The terms of the Cairo Declaration shall be carried out"; Japan undertakes "to carry out the provisions of the Potsdam Declaration in good faith" | binding undertaking of Japan; **surrender = cessation of hostilities, not end of the war** | an undertaking to act, not a cession; final disposition left to a peace treaty; the state of war continued until 1952-04-28 (L10) |
| L4 | 1945-09 | General Order No. 1 (P33) | Japanese forces in Formosa surrender to Chiang Kai-shek **for the Allied Powers** | ROC entry = acceptance of surrender on behalf of the Allies | — |

| L7 | 1946 | FRUS 1946 VIII docs 163, 204, 276 (P36, P37, P02) | US Nov 1946: Formosans "still retain Japanese nationality" under Japanese law; SWNCC 272/1: transfer "may eventually have to be formalized by appropriate treaty arrangements" | contested at the time | SWNCC also said "restored" |

| L9 | 1950-06-27 | Truman statement | "The determination of the future status of Formosa must await the restoration of security in the Pacific, a peace settlement with Japan, or consideration by the United Nations." | status undetermined in 1950 | — |
| L10 | 1952-04-28 | SFPT Art. 1(a), 2(b) (P04) | Art. 1(a) "The state of war between Japan and each of the Allied Powers is terminated as from the date on which the present Treaty comes into force"; Art. 2(b) "Japan renounces all right, title and claim to Formosa and the Pescadores." | **end of the war in law**; **no recipient** (contrast 2(a) Korea) | — |

**Conclusions this chain supports**:
- C1 No instrument 1945–1972 determined to whom Taiwan belongs (L10–L12, L14–L20).
- C2 Inhabitants' nationality was changed by unilateral acts and they were never given an option (L1, L5, L6, L7, L13).
- C3 Taipei Art. 10 confers no nationality; it is a purpose-limited fiction referring to ROC domestic law (L12).
