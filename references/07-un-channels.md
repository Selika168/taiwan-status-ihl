# 07 — UN and international channels for the inhabitants

## For Austronesian Indigenous Peoples' Organizations (IPOs)
- PFII: IPOs need **no** ECOSOC status; first-time accreditation via the iCSO form (mission, structure, focal point), then session registration in Indico; pre-registration typically Jan–Feb, closing 2–4 weeks before the April session (social.desa.un.org).
- Also EMRIP, UN Voluntary Fund for Indigenous Peoples, SR on the rights of Indigenous Peoples.
- Must be composed of and governed by indigenous peoples; others may assist only.

## Non-Self-Governing Territory (NSGT) listing
- Charter Art. 73 binds Members "which have or assume responsibilities for the administration" — none currently administers Taiwan (ROC not a Member since 1971; PRC does not administer).
- GA can decide over the administering State's objection: Res. 1542 (1960) on Portugal (P81). Criteria: Res. 1541 Principles IV–V ("geographically separate and … distinct ethnically and/or culturally from the country administering it"; "arbitrarily … in a position or status of subordination"); Principle VI full self-government = independence / free association / integration.
- Reinscription practice (French Polynesia, Res. 67/265, P82; New Caledonia, Res. 41/41): territory's own institutions request → regional/political group support → Member States table draft → GA "recognizes … remains a Non-Self-Governing Territory".
- C-24 hears petitioners only on agenda items (listed territories + Puerto Rico by its own decisions) (P83); its 29 members include China and Russia.

## Precedents for recognition gatekeeping
- Palestine: 1989 accession request — Swiss depositary "not … in a position to decide"; 1982 unilateral undertaking to apply GC IV accepted as valid; 2014 accession accepted after UNGA 67/19 (2012) (Wall para. 91, P74). ICC: 2009 Art. 12(3) declaration not acted on; 2015 accepted.
- ICJ Statute Art. 36(2) declarations are open only to parties to the Statute.
- The ICRC: an NGO's declaration produces no Convention effect; Protecting Powers are designated by the Parties (GC IV Art. 9), and when none functions the **Occupying/Detaining Power** must request a substitute (Art. 11) — the population need not apply.
